# RESTOK visual system

## Direction

Green pantry light: the quiet moment of opening a well-organized cupboard before leaving for the market. The visual language combines the warmth of a household note with the precision of a useful shopping tool.

The supplied `DESIGN (3).md` was used as a composition reference for editorial pacing, hairline borders, strong whitespace and product-as-hero. Its finance-specific dark terminal language was intentionally not copied.

## Tokens

- canvas/surface: `#FAFAF7`
- ink: `#171A18`
- muted: `#52645B`
- primary: `#174C3C`
- soft: `#B8D8C7`
- sage: `#EAF3EE`
- terracotta: `#963F36`
- line: `#D6DFD9`

All screens use bundled Geist and the approved raster brand mark. Reading uses regular/medium weights; semibold marks hierarchy. Dividers group operational content. One action bar reserves room above bottom navigation. Statuses pair text with iconography.

## Components

The main vocabulary is a compact shopping row, category disclosure, budget strip, bottom sheet, fixed purchase summary, and three-item app navigation. Primary actions are green; terracotta is reserved for budget overages and price increases.
